> **Legal Ethics Notice:** ABA Model Rule 1.6 (Confidentiality of Information) requires lawyers to make "reasonable efforts to prevent the inadvertent or unauthorized disclosure of, or unauthorized access to, information relating to the representation of a client."

### The Ethical Dilemma of Cloud Voice Dictation in Legal Practice

Attorneys and litigation teams draft millions of words every year: client intake memos, deposition summaries, trial briefs, motion filings, and settlement communications.

Voice dictation allows practitioners to write at 180–220 words per minute—nearly triple the speed of manual typing. However, adopting **cloud-based transcription apps** introduces severe ethical and evidentiary risks:

1. **Third-Party Disclosure:** Uploading unencrypted client statements to a cloud vendor's servers can be construed as voluntary disclosure to a third party, potentially waiving attorney-client privilege.
2. **Subpoena & Cloud Discovery:** Transcripts and audio files stored on a SaaS vendor's cloud database are subject to direct subpoenas and civil discovery requests without the law firm's advance notice.
3. **Model Training & Data Mining:** Cloud providers often reserve rights in their terms of service to utilize submitted audio to fine-tune AI models.

### How On-Device Architecture Protects Legal Privilege

HushWrite solves the confidentiality dilemma by eliminating the cloud entirely:

```
[Attorney Speaks]
       │

[Local Workstation RAM] ──(DirectML / Metal)──► [Local Whisper Neural Network]
       │                                                         │
       ▼                                                         ▼
[Audio Buffer Zeroized & Purged] ◄── [SendInput Types Text Into Word / Clio]
```

- **Zero Network Transit:** Audio never leaves your physical workstation.
- **Zero Third-Party Vendor Access:** No cloud sub-processors or cloud databases exist.
- **Immediate RAM Erasure:** Audio buffers exist purely in temporary system memory and are freed the instant text is typed.
- **Deterministic Auto-Purge:** Configure local history retention to 0 days (Incognito Mode) or immediate purge upon workstation lock.

### Practical Implementation for Law Firms

1. **Deploy Without IT Friction:** Install the standalone Windows/macOS binary without configuring cloud API keys or signing complex vendor BAAs.
2. **Enable Incognito Mode for Sensitive Matters:** For ultra-sensitive M&A deals or criminal defense matters, toggle Incognito Mode to disable local SQLite history logging completely.
3. **Verify with Network Monitoring:** Law firm IT teams can verify zero outbound network activity using Windows Packet Monitor or macOS LuLu.
