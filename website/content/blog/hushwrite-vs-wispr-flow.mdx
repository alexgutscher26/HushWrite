### Introduction: The Rise of Modern Push-to-Talk Dictation

Wispr Flow popularized the modern push-to-talk voice dictation workflow for knowledge workers. By holding a global hotkey, speaking, and releasing, users could bypass the clunkiness of legacy speech-to-text systems.

However, Wispr Flow is built on a **cloud-first architecture**: your audio is streamed over WebSockets to remote GPU clusters, free usage is capped at 2,000 words per week, and continued use requires a recurring ~$15/month ($180/year) subscription.

**HushWrite** was created to deliver that same polished push-to-talk experience—but executed **100% locally on your own computer's GPU**.

### Privacy & Corporate Compliance

For lawyers, doctors, and software engineers working under strict non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) or regulatory frameworks (HIPAA, GDPR), streaming voice drafts to cloud servers creates unneeded risk.

HushWrite guarantees that **no audio, transcripts, or telemetry ever leave your workstation**, making IT approval immediate and friction-free.
